Rank values: Scout — 299 gems · Ranger — 599 gems · Champion — 999 gems · Mythic — 1,499 gems. Upgrades charge the difference: Scout → Ranger is 300 gems, Scout → Champion is 700, and Scout → Mythic is 1,200. The server should subtract the value of the rank the player already owns, so they don’t pay for it twice. Each 100 gems is planned to equal $1. No payment checkout is connected yet.

Buy for myself

At checkout, enter your exact Minecraft username. After payment is confirmed, the server checks that your account has joined AdvaitMC before and adds the gems or Scout rank. Your purchase is delivered when you next join.

Gift to a player

Choose the gift option and enter the recipient’s exact Minecraft username. The recipient must have joined AdvaitMC at least once. The server delivers the gift to that player on their next login.
